Turn Everyday Forms Into Business Answers:
How to Maximize Your eForms with xNinja

INSIGHTS•24 June 2026•3 min read

For years, businesses struggled with paper forms, they are messy, hard to track, and incredibly easy to lose. The natural progression was to digitize, transforming those physical documents into eForms. But while transitioning to digital solves the clutter problem, many organizations are missing the bigger picture: Do you know that your submitted eForms can actually be powerful data?

Every form your team fills out is a piece of data, and xNinja turns that raw data into actionable insight. Forms shouldn't just be about filling in information, they also represent your operational data.

For Human Resources: Optimizing Employee Support

You can create a new eForm Report to track departmental trends and employee well-being. As HR professionals, your job is to analyze how employees are doing and determine how best to support them for the overall benefit of the company. You would typically ask questions like, “Which department takes the most leave?” With eForm Reports, you can answer this instantly by setting up a dedicated view:

  1. Set the Related eForm to "Leave eForms" under the "Personnel" category.
  2. Set the Default Range to "All."
  3. Generate the specific charts you need to visualize absence trends.
  4. Save the report and pin it to your homepage for quick daily updates.
Now, with xNinja Archive, you can also ask directly via our chat interface about the current data or query a specific employee's history to thoroughly analyze the trends.

For Sales: Tracking Pipeline and Performance

You can build a custom eForm Report to track your orders and monitor sales performance. For a sales team, the primary focus is on maximizing the conversion funnel and identifying top-performing assets. This leads to critical questions like, “Which product sells the most this quarter?” or “What is our quote-to-order rate by salesperson?” You can uncover these insights by configuring your report:

  1. Set the Report Title to "Quotation Report".
  2. Set the Default Range to "This Month."
  3. Generate bar graphs categorizing by products, quotes sent, orders
  4. Save the report and pin it to your homepage for quick daily updates.
Now, with xNinja Archive, you can seamlessly ask the chat interface to cross-reference specific client data or forecast next month's targets based on your historical order forms.

For Finance: Monitoring Departmental Expenses

You can set up an eForm Report to keep a close eye on company spending and budget allocation. For the finance department, maintaining a healthy bottom line means analyzing cash flow and asking questions like, "Which department has the highest reimbursement claims this month?" You can easily track this by building a custom dashboard:

  1. Set the Related eForm to "Expense Reimbursement Forms" under the "Finance" category.
  2. Set the Default Range to "This Month."
  3. Generate pie charts or bar graphs categorizing expenses by department, project, or individual.
  4. Save the report to your homepage to catch budget overruns before they happen.
Now, with xNinja, you can dive deeper by chatting directly with the system to isolate specific high-value claims or instantly compare this month's spending against previous quarters.
